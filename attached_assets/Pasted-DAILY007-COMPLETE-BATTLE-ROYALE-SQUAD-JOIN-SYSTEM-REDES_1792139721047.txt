2. PLAYER JOIN SYSTEM

Every authenticated user can select any available slot in any team and join individually.

When a player selects an empty slot:

1. Display the tournament entry fee in coins.
2. Show the selected team number and slot number.
3. Ask for confirmation before deducting coins.
4. Verify wallet balance on the server.
5. Deduct the entry fee from that player's wallet only.
6. Record the transaction in the wallet ledger.
7. Assign the player to the selected slot atomically.
8. Refresh the team card and available player count.

Example: If the entry fee is 10 coins, four different players can independently join Team 1 by paying 10 coins each. The team receives four players, and each player's wallet is charged only 10 coins.

If the entry fee is zero, joining must not deduct coins.

Prevent duplicate registrations, double charges, concurrent slot conflicts, and joining when the tournament is full or closed. If registration fails, ensure the player is not charged. Use database transactions or equivalent reliable atomic operations.

3. TEAM CAPTAIN SYSTEM

- The first player who successfully joins an empty team automatically becomes that team's Captain.
- Display a clear "CAPTAIN" badge beside their name.
- The Captain occupies one of the four player slots.
- The other three players can join the remaining available slots independently.
- A team must never have more than one active Captain.
- Store captain status in the database, not just in the UI.

The Captain must be able to remove a teammate from their own team before the tournament registration lock time.

When the Captain selects a teammate's Remove option:

1. Display a confirmation dialog.
2. Verify on the server that the requester is the current Captain of that team.
3. Verify that the tournament registration is still open.
4. Remove only the selected teammate from that team.
5. Release the player's slot for another player.
6. Update the team count and available slots immediately.

IMPORTANT: The Captain cannot remove themselves using the teammate removal action. The Captain cannot remove players from another team. Regular teammates cannot remove other players.

Removing a teammate must NOT automatically refund coins. Keep the entry fee charged unless the existing, explicitly configured tournament refund policy requires otherwise. Clearly communicate the policy before removal.

If the Captain leaves voluntarily or their registration is cancelled, transfer Captain status to an eligible remaining teammate automatically. If no teammates remain, the next player who joins becomes Captain.
