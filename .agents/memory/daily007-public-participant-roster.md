---
name: Daily007 public participant roster
description: Privacy rule for participant identities shown on tournament details.
---

**Rule:** Public tournament rosters show team names and Free Fire in-game names only. Do not expose account IDs, emails, game UIDs, or Daily007 application profile names. Include active approved and pending registrations, and identify pending entries as awaiting approval.

**Why:** The user wants players and teams to see who joined without exposing account or contact details.

**How to apply:** Public roster endpoints and detail screens should use captain-first game-name ordering, include teammates in roster order, and exclude rejected registrations.
