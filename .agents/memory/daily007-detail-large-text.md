---
name: Daily007 tournament details at large text
description: Keeps tournament-detail summaries, room credentials, and squad slots readable at enlarged Android text sizes.
---

For tournament details at 1.5× text and above, stack room credential values above their copy actions at every phone width, and wrap four-slot squad rows into two columns on narrow screens. Keep labels and actions fully visible instead of truncating them.

**Why:** Width-only breakpoints miss enlarged text on common phone widths, while four side-by-side player slots leave too little space for names and controls.

**How to apply:** Use the platform font scale for detail-layout decisions and verify solo and team details at both 1.5× and 2× on Android.
