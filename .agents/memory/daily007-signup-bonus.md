name: Daily007 signup bonus
description: User-confirmed policy for the one-time welcome reward and referral signup reward.
---

Every new Daily007 account receives 10 coins once at account creation. A referred signup's existing 10-coin referral bonus fulfills this total; do not stack it to 20 coins. Existing accounts are not automatically credited on later login.

**Why:** The user chose a total of 10 coins for every new user, including referral signups, rather than a separate stacking bonus.

**How to apply:** Keep every account-creation path on the shared signup-reward logic and issue the credit inside the signup transaction. Do not backfill existing accounts on login; handle any requested retroactive credit through an explicit admin adjustment.
